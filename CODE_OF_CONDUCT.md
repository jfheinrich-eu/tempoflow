# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

TempoFlow contributors and maintainers must communicate respectfully, discuss technical work in good faith, welcome constructive disagreement, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publication of private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, and other participation that violates this standard. They may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants according to severity and recurrence.

Report conduct concerns privately to `contact@jfheinrich.eu`. Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical. Reports concerning a maintainer will be handled by another available maintainer.
